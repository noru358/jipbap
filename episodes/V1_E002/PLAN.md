## Episode concept

Menu: 김치볶음밥 + 반숙 계란후라이
Core proxy-eater payoff: 독자가 직접 먹는 대신 인물이 노른자를 터뜨리고, 매콤한 김치볶음밥을 노른자에 비벼 한입 먹어주는 순간을 감각적으로 대신 경험시킨다.

Why this moment supports six beats:
- intact egg gives a clear before-state
- yolk break creates a visible transformation
- mixing changes color/texture
- crisped rice edge gives a secondary texture payoff
- spoon-to-mouth action gives an unambiguous ingestion beat
- second-scoop impulse supplies a natural aftertaste ending without forced punchline

## BODY storyboard

### S01 — 딱 봐도 먼저 건드릴 게 보이는 상태
Story function:

Visual:

Camera / expression:

- small anticipatory grin; shoulders subtly forward.

Copy draft:

Continuity state:

- rice still mostly unmixed with egg.

### S02 — 노른자 터지는 순간
Story function:
- preparation / transformation

Visual:
- spoon edge or spoon bowl presses the yolk.
- membrane breaks and yolk just begins to flow across the rice.
- hand/spoon/yolk contact must be physically clear.

Camera / expression:
- tight food/action crop, more top-down than S01.
- face may be omitted; action itself carries the beat.

Copy draft:
- silent.
- deterministic SFX later may use a restrained `톡` if layout benefits.

Continuity state:
- yolk newly broken, flow only beginning.

### S03 — 비비는 순간 맛이 바뀜
Story function:
- transformation / sensory reveal

Visual:
- spoon folds yolk through a portion of kimchi rice.
- some grains become yellow-orange while some red rice remains visible.
- a few browned/crisped grains at the pan edge are visible as a later payoff cue.

Camera / expression:
- close side relation on spoon + rice, character only partially present if useful.
- hand action is more important than face.

Copy draft:
- inner thought: `와, 노른자 섞이니까 매운맛이 확 부드러워지네.`

Continuity state:
- egg partly mixed, not uniformly blended.
- crisped edge remains available.

### S04 — 제대로 한 숟갈 뜨기
Story function:
- approach / sensory setup

Visual:
- heaped spoonful contains yolk-coated rice, kimchi pieces, and a little browned edge rice.
- character brings spoon upward; bowl/pan remains readable below.

Camera / expression:
- lower / near-spoon perspective so the bite feels close to the reader.
- face sits behind or beside the spoon with focused anticipation, not a repeated neutral portrait.

Copy draft:
- speech or thought: `이거 눌은 데까지 같이 떠야 돼.`

Continuity state:
- spoonful is a physically plausible scoop from S03 state.
- spoon has not reached the mouth yet.

### S05 — 한입
Story function:
- ingestion / immediate reaction

Visual:
- spoon reaches the mouth and the bite is actually taken.
- avoid ambiguous “near-mouth but not eating” staging.
- cheek/eyes can react modestly stronger than neutral realism.

Camera / expression:
- close 3/4 or side eating view, but not the same relation as S01.
- slight eye-widen / pleased cheek tension from heat + texture; no exaggerated anime reaction.

Copy draft:
- speech fragment: `앗 뜨거.`
- inner thought: `근데 김치는 아삭하고 밥알은 고슬고슬해서 식감 좋다.`

Continuity state:
- the S04 spoonful is now being consumed.

### S06 — 먹고 끝이 아니라 바로 다음 숟갈
Story function:
- aftertaste / secondary payoff / next-bite impulse

Visual:
- first bite is gone; character is chewing while the hand already returns to scoop again.
- pan/bowl shows reduced rice and yolk more widely mixed than before.
- no forced “잘 먹었다” pose.

Camera / expression:
- open the frame back up enough to show face, hand, and food relationship.
- relaxed satisfaction with a small “already going back in” urgency.

Copy draft:
- inner thought: `음... 노른자 고소한 맛 남아있다가 김치 맛 다시 올라온다.`
- small speech/reaction: `한 입 더.`

Continuity state:
- first bite completed.
- second scoop begins naturally from the already-mixed rice.

## Visual rhythm intent

The board should feel like one eating moment tightening and releasing:
- begin with food + character relationship,
- compress into yolk/contact and mixing detail,
- push the spoon toward the reader,
- land on the actual bite,
- reopen for the second-scoop aftertaste.

This is not a shot-type quota. Camera side, face direction and exact crop remain fluid at render time as long as the six cells do not collapse into repetitive same-angle portrait staging.

Expression arc:
- anticipation → concentration → appetite focus → immediate heat/pleasure → relaxed second-bite impulse.
Expressions may be modestly amplified for readability but must remain inside the locked drawing language.

## Copy refinement check

Copy should sound like someone actually talking to themselves while eating, not compressed written prose.
Preferred cadence:
- spoken endings such as `~해야지`, `~네`, `~좋다`, short reactions and pauses
- short sensory remarks tied to the current bite
- no report-like `-함 / -됨 / -임` cadence in dialogue or inner monologue unless a specific character voice genuinely calls for it
- community/thread flavor may appear through natural rhythm, not sentence-ending gimmicks

Concrete bite information included:
- yolk softens perceived kimchi heat
- kimchi stays crisp
- rice remains separate / 고슬고슬
- browned edge adds texture
- yolk richness lingers before kimchi aftertaste returns
- the aftertaste causes the next-scoop impulse

Final wording remains editable after storyboard approval.

## Physical / semantic continuity

Required only:
1. S01 intact yolk.
2. S02 yolk breaks.
3. S03 partial mixing follows the break.
4. S04 spoonful is scooped from that mixed state.
5. S05 that spoonful reaches and enters the mouth.
6. S06 first bite is complete and a second scoop begins.

Bridge motions may happen between cells, but no panel may contradict these before/after states.
